FIELD GUIDE — HOW TO PLAY

Explore: Travel across one continuous forest world housing 20 distinct math scroll clearings. Ahead of your current clearing, gates remain locked until you advance.

World Map: Press M or click MAP to view the full forest map, showing your exact position, cleared scrolls, and your active target scroll.

Movement: W / ↑ move north, S / ↓ move south, A / ← move west, D / → move east.

Scrolls: Press E when near a scroll to take on the calculus question.

Combat: Face an enemy and press SPACE to attack. Dodge incoming wind-up attacks. You are healed after every fight.

Boss Upgrades: Defeating each realm boss grants +5 Max HP and unlocks new abilities (Golden Greatsword, Homing Magic Darts on Q, and Magic Arcane Bombs on R).
